# Web app changelog

What changed in Groovekeeper on the web (groovekeeper.app), for the people using it. The web app has
no version numbers: it updates itself whenever a change is deployed, so everyone is always on the
latest, and each entry here is headed by the day it went live. The desktop app has its own changelog
in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 2026-10-10

### Added
- Settings: choose how chords are written, the size of the text in the editor, and what an export
  starts with. They are kept on your device, also when you aren't signed in.
- Do Re Mi: show and type chords as Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si, and choose letters, Do Re Mi or Roman
  numerals when you export.
- Settings → About links to the desktop app's installer.

### Changed
- The privacy page says what we see about visits (only the totals Cloudflare keeps) and when an
  individual account is looked at.

## 2026-10-09

### Added
- Groovekeeper is on the web at groovekeeper.app: write chord sheets, transpose, keep notes over a song,
  build setlists and export songs, setlists and songbooks as PDF. Your songs stay in your browser, and
  the app can be installed and used without a connection.
- Accounts: create one to have the same songs on every device, with a settings page and an account
  menu. Emails (confirm your address, reset your password) come in Groovekeeper's words.
- Your data is yours: export the whole library, or delete your account and everything in it.
